# Maurizio Dècina — content-preserving mockup revision

This revision supersedes the previous English marketing concept. The user requires the original website's complete content, wording and languages to remain the same, including on mobile.

## Content rules

- The live website is the content authority: https://mauriziodecina.it/.
- Do not translate Italian content into English, or English content into Italian.
- Preserve both Biography and Biografia, plus Publications.
- Preserve original names, headings, punctuation, accent marks, course names, academic years, dates, email addresses, document labels, file types, file-size details and link destinations.
- Preserve original typos and historical wording rather than silently correcting or modernising them.
- Do not replace the original introduction with a promotional headline or biography summary.
- Do not add Research, Career, View CV, institution strips, milestones or recognition blocks from the previous design/PDF when they are absent from the original homepage.
- Preserve all ten teaching periods in their original order: 2011–2012, 2010–2011, 2009–2010, 2008–2009, 2007–2008, 2006–2007, 2005–2006, 2003–2004, 2003, 2001.
- Preserve all 493 original homepage anchor entries, including duplicated and empty entries, in the source inventory. Do not silently discard historical materials or alter download destinations.
- Use the original source portrait. The visual concepts restyle its presentation; the original image remains available for eventual implementation.
- Mobile changes layout, sizing, wrapping and spacing; content and language remain the same.
- The PDF supplies visual inspiration only. Its example text must not override the live website.
