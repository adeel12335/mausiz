# Implementation design system
Active concepts: design/01-homepage-v2.png, design/02-course-materials-v2.png, design/03-mobile-v2.png.
Content authority: exact original homepage, both biographies, and publications captured under design/source-content.
Palette: white #fff; navy #10263d; gold #b59148; muted navy #526580; rules #dfe6ed; portrait backdrop #eef2f5.
Type: Georgia/Times serif for wordmark, display, navigation and document titles; readable serif body matching revised mockups. Header 42px at 1536; hero name 96px; affiliation 58px; archive heading 52px; document title 24px and metadata 18px at desktop. Responsive optical scaling with comfortable touch targets.
Layout: 72px desktop gutters, 92px header, 630px hero, left content and right photographic panel, two full-width featured resource rows, open archive list with 280px year rail and thin dividers. Square gold-outline biography links; gold download icons. Mobile stacked content/photo and document rows, collapsible primary nav.
Photo treatment: original identity, standalone photo/background asset from ImageGen, no color tint on person. Original unmodified source photo remains locally available.
Component families: site header/mobile menu, hero, biography navigation, featured resource rows, archive rail, original document rows, article typography.
All copy is taken from the original pages; no promotional copy, invented research sections, CV button, awards, footer claims or translated labels.
Interactions: biography/publication routes, responsive navigation, teaching-year anchors, document downloads, email links.
Content verification: compare normalized main-content text and original anchor order/destinations against source snapshots. Local paths may replace first-party destinations when the downloaded asset exists; unavailable files retain the original address.
