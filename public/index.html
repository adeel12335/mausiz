Biography

Engineer, professor and public servant: five decades spent building the digital networks of Italy and Europe, and teaching the engineers who run them.

Full curriculum vitae Curriculum vitae in italiano

Maurizio Dècina was born in Pescasseroli, in the province of L’Aquila, on 10 January 1943. He studied at the Liceo Classico Torquato Tasso in Rome and graduated in Electronic Engineering from the University of Rome in 1966. His first research, at Fondazione Ugo Bordoni, concerned the digital encoding of telephone signals and digital transmission over copper cables. He then managed the engineering of digital transmission systems at SIP, the company that is today Telecom Italia.

From 1972 to 1988 he was Vice Chairman of CCITT Study Group XVIII in Geneva, the committee that wrote the international Recommendations for digital networks and ISDN. He became full professor at the University of Rome La Sapienza in 1980, spent a sabbatical year at Bell Laboratories in Chicago on the Fast Packet Switching test-bed, and from 1983 to 1987 directed the R&D laboratories of Italtel, where some 800 people developed the Linea UT family of digital switching equipment.

In 1987 he moved to Politecnico di Milano as Professor of Telecommunications. The following year he founded CEFRIEL, the research and education consortium between the university and the ICT industry, and directed it until 2003. He was President of the IEEE Communications Society in 1994 and 1995, Editor in Chief of European Transactions on Telecommunications, and President of Fondazione Ugo Bordoni in 2007 and 2008.

He left his chair in July 2012 to serve as Commissioner of AGCOM, the Italian Communications Authority, and in 2016 became President of Infratel Italia, the company that carries out the national broadband plans. Politecnico di Milano named him Professor Emeritus in 2014. He is an IEEE Fellow and holds the IEEE Award in International Communications, the IEEE Third Millennium Medal and the IEEE ComSoc/KICS Exemplary Global Service Award.

Career

A career that moved between research laboratories, industry and the standards bodies that defined digital networks, from the first PCM systems to the security of the Internet.

Full curriculum vitae Curriculum vitae in italiano

  1. 1966

    Dott. Ing. in Electronic Engineering, University of Rome

    After the Classic Lyceum Torquato Tasso in Rome, 1960.

  2. 1967 to 1969

    Researcher, Fondazione Ugo Bordoni, Rome

    Early research on the digital encoding of telephone signals and on baseband digital transmission over copper cables.

  3. 1969 to 1976

    Manager, New Techniques department, SIP, Rome

    Engineering of digital transmission systems for telephony and data at the headquarters of the company that is today Telecom Italia.

  4. 1972 to 1988

    Vice Chairman, CCITT Study Group XVIII, Geneva

    Sixteen years in the standards body that is today ITU-T, responsible for digital transmission, voice and video codecs, packet data networks and telephone signalling.

  5. 1976 to 1983

    Full Professor, University of Rome La Sapienza

    Joined the INFOCOM Department in 1976 and held the chair of Telephone and Telegraph Technology from 1980. Research on the digitalisation of the telephone network and on fast packet communications.

  6. 1981 to 1982

    Scientific consultant, Bell Telephone Laboratories, Chicago

    A sabbatical year on the Fast Packet Switching test-bed. Returned in 1989 and 1990 for the free-space photonic Optical Cross-Connect test-bed.

  7. 1983 to 1987

    Executive Director of R&D, Italtel, Milan

    Coordinated a laboratory of about 800 people developing the Linea UT family of digital switching equipment with ISDN and data features. It came to serve about 70% of Italian wired telephony customers.

  8. 1987 to 2012

    Professor of Telecommunications, Politecnico di Milano

    Chairs in Circuit theory, Switching systems, Telecommunications networks and Telematics, then a decade of courses on Internet infrastructure, cryptography and network security.

  9. 1988 to 2003

    Founder and Scientific Director, CEFRIEL, Milan

    A research and education consortium with the major information and telecommunications companies as partners. Its R&D budget reached 6 million euro in 2002.

  10. 1991 to 1997

    Editor in Chief, European Transactions on Telecommunications

    The international technical journal published in collaboration between the countries of the European Community under EUREL and AEI.

  11. 1994 to 1995

    President, IEEE Communications Society, New York

    Elected to lead a society of 40,000 members with a budget of more than 25 million dollars a year. Drove its internationalisation and the move of journals and conferences onto Internet technology.

  12. 2007 to 2008

    President, Fondazione Ugo Bordoni, Rome

    Research and consultancy for the Communications Minister and the Italian Communications Authority. Reorganised the research areas and introduced new bylaws to consolidate the Foundation's mission.

  13. 2012

    Commissioner, AGCOM, Italian Communications Authority

    Left the Politecnico chair in July 2012 to join the board of the Authority he had advised since 1999.

  14. 2014

    Professor Emeritus, Politecnico di Milano

    Named Professor Emeritus by the university where he had taught for twenty-five years.

  15. 2016

    President, Infratel Italia

    Appointed by the shareholders’ meeting in October 2016 to chair the Ministry of Economic Development’s company for national broadband infrastructure.

Research

Four threads run through 128 selected publications, from the planning of the first digital hierarchies to the security of Internet infrastructure.

Complete list of selected publications

1968 to 1979

Digital transmission and PCM hierarchies

Early work at Fondazione Ugo Bordoni and SIP on the digital encoding of telephone signals, PCM multiplex transmission over coaxial and copper cables, and the planning of the digital hierarchies that carried them.

  • Planning of a Digital HierarchyIEEE Transactions on Communications, 1972
  • Character Alignment for High Capacity PCM Systems using MS43 Line CodeIEEE Transactions on Communications, 1975
  • Reframing Statistics of PCM Multiplex TransmissionElectronics Letters, 1969

1979 to 1988

ISDN and international standards

Sixteen years as Vice Chairman of CCITT Study Group XVIII, shaping the Recommendations for customer access, signalling and speech coding that became the Integrated Services Digital Network.

  • Customer Access Protocols to Integrated Services Digital NetworksComputer Communications, 1979
  • CCITT Recommendations on ISDN: A ReviewIEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, 1986
  • Traffic Engineering for ISDN Design and PlanningNorth-Holland, 1988, edited with M. Bonatti

1981 to 1997

Fast packet switching, ATM and broadband

From the Fast Packet Switching test-bed at Bell Laboratories to the Italian ATM test-bed and shuffle interconnection networks: switching architectures for voice, data and video on a single network.

  • Voice by the Packet?IEEE Journal on Selected Areas in Communications, 1983
  • ATM or STM or both?IEEE Communications Magazine, 1991
  • Multistage Shuffle Networks with Shortest Path and Deflection Routing for High Performance ATM SwitchingIEEE Transactions on Communications, 1994

1997 to 2012

Internet, convergence and network security

The convergence of telecommunications and computing, voice over IP and electronic commerce, then a decade of research and teaching on Internet infrastructure, cryptography and network security at Politecnico di Milano.

  • Convergence of Telecommunications and Computing to Networking Models for Integrated Services and ApplicationsProceedings of the IEEE, 1997
  • Voice over Internet Protocol and Human assisted Electronic CommerceIEEE Communications Magazine, 1999
  • Electronic Commerce: Enablers and ImplicationsIEEE Communications Magazine, 1999
