A Career Across Research, Industry & Institutions

More than fifty-five years in telecommunications, from research and teaching to international standards and the regulation of networks. One of the fathers of high-speed packet switching, the work that opened the way to today’s Internet.

Research & Innovation

Contributions to digital transmission, switching, network architectures, interoperability and security.

Learn more

Industry Leadership

Leadership roles at Bell Laboratories, Italtel, CEFRIEL and on the boards of major ICT companies, bridging research and real-world innovation.

Explore career

Public Institutions

Commissioner of AGCOM from 2012 to 2013, President of Infratel Italia from 2016 to 2020, and expert consultant to the Minister for Technological Innovation and Digital Transition from 2021 to 2022.

Key Milestones

  1. 1966

    Graduated in Electronic Engineering, University of Rome

    Full Professor, University of Rome La Sapienza

  3. 1981–82 · 1990

    Scientific consultant, Bell Laboratories

  4. 1986

    Professor of Telecommunications, Politecnico di Milano

  5. 1986 – 2003

    Founder and Scientific Director, CEFRIEL

  6. 1994 – 1995

    President, IEEE Communications Society

  7. 2012 – 2013

    Commissioner, AGCOM

    President, Infratel Italia

    Expert consultant to the Minister for Technological Innovation

Research areas

Fields That Defined a Connected Era

From fundamental research to global standards, contributing to the networks that connect people and societies.

Digital Transmission

Digital encoding of telephone signals, PCM multiplex transmission and the planning of digital hierarchies.

Explore this area

ISDN & Standards

Sixteen years as Vice Chairman of CCITT Study Group XVIII, shaping the Recommendations behind ISDN.

Explore this area

Packet Switching & ATM

From the Fast Packet Switching test-bed at Bell Laboratories to ATM switching for voice, data and video.

Explore this area

Internet & Network Security

Convergence of telecommunications and computing, Internet infrastructure, cryptography and network security.
